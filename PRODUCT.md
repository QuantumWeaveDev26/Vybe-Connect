# VYBE Connect

A hosted, installable web application for team CRM, customer conversations, campaigns, automation and workspace AI. Teammates use their own invited accounts. Customer workspaces remain isolated and require their own subscription.

The approved internal workspaces bmai and QuantumWeave AI share one INR 299 monthly AI budget. Agent findings require human review; configuration and scheduling are separate. Credentials remain server-side.

WhatsApp API messaging requires an eligible connected number and provider acceptance. Manual click-to-chat opens the user's existing WhatsApp application and does not synchronize history or delivery states. Offline mode does not queue customer sends or edits. Preserve these evidence and permission boundaries during UI changes.
