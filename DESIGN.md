# VYBE Connect design

User-approved reference, 25 November 2026: a white conversation workspace with a slim light icon rail, quiet grey surfaces, violet controls, a conversation list, focused message area and contact details pane. The same palette applies to every module, authentication and the opening animation.

Operate mode. Body text is neutral charcoal; violet identifies actions and selection. Green, amber and red remain semantic status colours. Use compact 8–12px corners, thin separators and restrained elevation. Preserve the VYBE identity and existing real agent portraits; do not copy the reference's customer names, messages, activity counts or product logo.

Desktop inbox: three panes when space allows. Intermediate screens: list plus conversation with explicit contact details. Phones: one pane at a time, labelled back navigation, a scrollable message area and visible composer. Touch controls remain reachable; input text avoids mobile zoom. Other pages keep the same shared controls and responsive record layouts.

Controls must have actual effects. Reply and private notes remain distinct. Manual WhatsApp handoff does not simulate synchronized messages. Assignment and resolve actions use existing workspace permission checks. Provider-dependent buttons retain their connection and approval gates. Team AI retains the shared INR 299 monthly ceiling.
