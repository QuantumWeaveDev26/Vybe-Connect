Request data deletion

Email team.quantumweaveai@gmail.com with the subject “VYBE Connect data deletion”.

  1. Identify the workspace or business involved.
  2. Tell us which account or contact record concerns you and what you want deleted. Include the associated email or WhatsApp number only if needed to locate the record.
  3. Send the request from your account email when possible. We may ask for additional verification before taking action.

Never send passwords, API keys or authentication codes. Requests are reviewed manually; submitting an email does not immediately erase records. We will explain the outcome and any retention needed for security or legal obligations.

For messages sent by another business, you may also contact that business directly. Deletion from VYBE Connect does not remove independent copies held by message recipients or connected providers.
