VYBE Connect

Privacy notice

Updated 5 November 2026

VYBE Connect is a business messaging workspace operated by QuantumWeave AI. Contact team.quantumweaveai@gmail.com about this notice or your personal information.

Information processed

The service stores account details, password hashes, workspace membership, contacts, conversation messages and media, delivery events, and records you choose to add, such as knowledge documents and orders. It also stores service configuration and activity records needed to operate and secure the workspace.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, display and manage business conversations, deliver configured messaging and automation, support customer requests, and protect workspace access. Businesses using a workspace are responsible for their customer notices, permissions and lawful use of customer data.

Connected services

WhatsApp messages and related events pass through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. When AI is enabled and used, relevant prompts and selected workspace content are sent to the configured AI provider, including OpenAI where selected. Other providers receive relevant information only when their integrations are configured and used. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and access

Workspace records are stored on the service's hosting infrastructure. Authorized workspace members can access records according to their roles. Service operators may access information to maintain the service, investigate problems, and respond to verified requests. Session cookies maintain sign-in; API credentials are kept on the server and are not intended for public display.

Retention and deletion

Records may remain while the workspace is in use. Removing an integration does not automatically delete existing workspace records. To request account or personal-data deletion, follow our data-deletion instructions. Some records may need to be retained for security, dispute handling or legal obligations. Copies held independently by Meta, other providers or message recipients are subject to their own policies.

Your choices

Contact the business that sent you a message to update your preferences or stop its messages. For access, correction, deletion or questions about data held by VYBE Connect, contact the address above. We verify the requester's authority before disclosing or removing private data.
